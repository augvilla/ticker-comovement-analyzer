# Ticker Co-Movement Analyzer

A terminal-styled tool for comparing how two tickers move together over time.

Pick any two stocks, ETFs, or crypto assets and a date range, and the app
shows:

- **Side-by-side price charts** for both tickers over that period
- **Co-movement score** — the percentage of trading days both tickers moved
  in the same direction (both up or both down)

For example, if Stock A goes up every day and Stock B only goes up on half
of those days, the co-movement score comes out to 0.50.

It's a quick way to see whether two assets tend to move together, move
opposite each other, or show no clear relationship at all — useful for
thinking about diversification, hedging, or just understanding how
different parts of a portfolio behave relative to one another.

## Built with

Python, Streamlit, and live market data.

## Access

This app is private and limited to approved users.
